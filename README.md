# Trivandrum — a city coming to life

An animated Blender miniature of Thiruvananthapuram, with rising landmarks, a red TRIVANDRUM rooftop reveal, moving trains, an aircraft taking off, and road traffic.

## Open in Blender

Download [the editable Blender project](outputs/Trivandrum-Closer-1080p60.blend) and open it in Blender 5.2.1 LTS or a compatible newer version. Material images and the soundtrack are packed into the project. Build scripts are retained in Blender's text datablocks.

The main scene is `TRIVANDRUM • Photo reconstruction`. The timeline spans frames 1–1080 at 60 fps. This version uses closer camera framing, with a gradual push-in and less surrounding empty ground.

## Places and motion

The composition includes Padmanabhaswamy Temple, Kuthira Malika, Napier Museum and Zoo, Kanakakunnu Palace, Kovalam, Shanghumukham, Poovar, Ponmudi, Neyyar sanctuary and dam, Technopark, K-DISC at India Heights, Palayam's temple–mosque–church cluster, the Secretariat, Vizhinjam Port, State Central Library, Thampanoor bus terminal, the airport, Greenfield Stadium, LuLu Mall, the Legislative Assembly, LMS Church, and public statues.

Buildings rise in staggered groups. Two passenger trains travel on separate elevated tracks; cars, motorbikes and bicycles move through signal-controlled roads. The airport includes a departing aircraft and a parked aircraft.

This is a stylized, condensed miniature with approximate architecture and geography, not an exact or hyperrealistic reconstruction. No personal employment dates appear in the film.
